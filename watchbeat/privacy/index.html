Privacy policy

Support

Effective November 8, 2026

WatchBeat does not collect your data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't send anything over the internet. Everything it does happens on your iPhone.

Microphone

WatchBeat uses the microphone only while it's measuring, to hear your watch tick. The sound is analyzed on your iPhone.

The most recent recording is kept temporarily, in the app's private temporary storage, while a result or error screen is showing, so that you can choose to include it in a debug report. It is deleted as soon as you leave that screen, and anything left over is deleted the next time the app starts.

Motion sensors

WatchBeat uses the iPhone's motion sensors to tell which way the phone is being held: to keep the display upright, and to recognize each position during a Position Study. Motion readings are used as they happen and are never stored or sent.

What's stored on your iPhone

Only your lift-angle setting. Deleting the app removes it.

Debug reports, only if you send one

If a reading looks wrong, you can tap Send Debug to email or share a report. A report contains:

  • the recording, which can include other sounds in the room, such as voices;
  • the measurement details;
  • the app version and your iOS version;
  • any watch name and notes you choose to type.

Nothing leaves your iPhone unless you send it yourself through Mail or the share sheet, and it goes only where you send it.

Reports sent to the developer are used to diagnose problems and improve WatchBeat's measurements, and may be kept as test recordings. To have a report you sent deleted, email the address below.

Children

WatchBeat isn't directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone.
